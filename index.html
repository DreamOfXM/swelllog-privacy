SwellLog — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 8, 2026

SwellLog does not collect any personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. Everything you record — surf sessions, boards, conditions — stays in the app's storage on your device.

What the app stores

Your surf log and quiver are saved in the app's local document storage on your device only. They never leave the device unless you explicitly use a share or export button, in which case the shared text goes to whatever app you choose (e.g. Mail or Messages), under that app's own policy.

Purchases

The one-time unlock (Full Quiver) is processed entirely by Apple. We receive no billing details. Purchase state is restored from your Apple Account.

Feedback

If you email us, we use your address only to reply. It is never added to any list.
